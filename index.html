Project 1

Title

E-commerce Order Analysis: View Excel Dashboard

Project Description

This project analyzes E-commerce sales performance using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard was built to identify sales patterns, customer behavior, and top performing product. The dashboard include the following features:

  • Total quantity by product: A breakdown of how many quantities sold by each product.
  • Payment method: This compares the different payment method customers used in making payment.
  • Total revenue by product: Highlights the total revenue generated by each product.
  • Referral sources: Analyze the different customer acquisition channels.
  • Order statues by quantity and product: This explains how order statue affects different product and their quantity.

Tools Used

  • Microsoft Excel
  • Pivot Tables
  • Pivot Charts
  • Slicers
  • Timeline

Key Findings

  • Best Selling Products: Printers, tables and chair recorded the highest order quantities and generated strong revenue compared to other products.
  • Payment Preference: Online payments were the most commonly used payment method, indicating a growing preference for convenient digital transactions among customers.
  • Order Status Insight: A noticeable number of cancelled and returned orders were identified, highlighting areas where delivery efficiency and customer satisfaction could be improved.
  • Customer Acquisition Channels: Social media and referral platforms contributed significantly to customer traffic, showing the importance of digital marketing channels in driving sales.
  • Product Demand Patterns: Certain products consistently performed better across different order statuses, helping identify high demand items and supporting smarter inventory planning.

This dashboard provide valuable business insight that can support decision making in sales monitoring, customer behavior analysis, marketing strategy and operation improvement.

Title

E-commerce Sales & Customer Behavior: View Power BI Dashboard

Project Description

This project analyze transaction and behavioral data from Turkey's E-commerce sector to uncover the relationship between customer activity, purchasing patterns and revenue performance in the year 2023-2024. The dashboard allows stakeholder to identify high performing categories, understand customer purchasing habits and track the metrics that matter most in a competitive E-commerce environment. The dashboard include the following features:

  • Total Sales by Month: A monthly breakdown of the total sales, providing insight into sales trends over time.
  • Revenue by Product: This highlighted the total revenue generated by each product.
  • Revenue by Cities: It shows the total revenue generated by each cities, showcasing performance in different market.
  • Payment Method: This compares the different payment method used by the customers.
  • Delivery Time Days: Displays how long/days it takes to deliver products.
  • Customer Rating: Highlights the rating of the customer from their shopping experience.
    • Additionally, the dashboard include interactive slicer and KPIs:
  • Year: Filter data to view performance for a specific year.
  • Product Category: Drill down into the performance of each product.
  • Gender: Filter data to view performance of each gender.
  • Total Revenue: Calculated the total revenue generated from the sales of products.
  • Total Customer and Quantity: Calculated the total number of customers and the total quantities sold.

Tools Used

  • Power BI
  • Power Query
  • Dax
  • Kpi Tracking
  • Data Modeling
  • Data Visualization

Key Findings

  • The E-commerce market generated over ₺5M in revenue, with Electronics emerging as the strongest performing category.
  • Istanbul recorded the highest sales contribution, reinforcing its position as the primary commercial hub.
  • A significant percentage of customers were returning buyer, indicating strong customer retention and repeat purchase activity.
  • Delivery timelines were largely concentrated within 5-7 days showing relatively stable fulfillment performance across the cities.
  • More than half of all purchase were completed through mobile devices, showing a clear shift toward mobile driven shopping behavior.

This dashboard shows a business focused report solution designed to support strategic decisions in marketing, customer engagement and operational planning.

Project 5

Title

Mental Health and Social Media Balance: View Power BI Dashboard

Project Description

This power BI project is a behavioral analytics report examining how 500 users across 6 social media platform interact with digital technology and downstream effect on lifestyle indications like sleep,exercise and screen time. The dashboard include the following features:

  • Number of Users by Screen Time(hr): Hours of time users spends on social media daily.
  • Number of Users by Age Group: Different age group and their social media usage.
  • Number of User by Days Without Social Media: Visual representation of how many days users can stay without social media.
  • Number of User by Social Media: This highlights the most frequent social media platform users visits.
  • Number of User by Sleep Quanlity: Displays how many hours of sleep social media users gets daily
  • Number of User by Exercise(per week): This shows how frequent social media user exercise weekly

Tools Used

  • Power BI
  • Power query
  • Dax
  • Kpi Tracking
  • Data Visualization

Key Findings

  • Heavy Screen Time Dominates: The majority of users 256 fall in the 4-6hrs/day followed by 204 in the 7-10hrs/day, while Only 40 users report 1-3hrs/day. This means over 92% of the user uses social media for 4+hrs daily, which is a siginificant mental health risk indicator.
  • TikTok Leads Platform Usage: TikTok(95) edges out X/Twitter(88) and LinkedIn(87),with Instagram at bottom(74). This narrow spread suggests users are distributed fairly evenly, though TIkTok and X/Twitter may warrant closer scrutiny given their algorithmically driven and high engagement design.
  • Exercise Frequency is Low for Most Users: The exercise curve peaks at 2-3 sessions/per week(132 and 117 respectively), but drops off significantly beyond 4 sessions. This shows a large portion of users exercise infrequently which when combined with screen time suggests a sedentary lifestyle .
  • Sleep Quality Peaks at 6hrs: The sleep quality curve rises steeply from 2hrs to peak at 120 users around the 6hrs mark, then drops sharply Very few user report 10hrs sleep, suggesting most users cluster around moderate sleep duration potentially disrupted by late night screen use.
  • Most Users Rarely Disconnect: 284 user(57%) went without social for only 0-3 days, while 15 user managed 7-10 days. This points to a high dependency pattern across the users
This dashboard paints a picture of a digitally dependent population with moderate wellness habit. The most actionable story is the mismatch between high screen time/low disconnection rate and the modest sleep and exercise metrics this suggests that social media usage is likely crowding out healthier behaviors.
