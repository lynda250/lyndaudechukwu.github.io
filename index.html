Project 1: E-commerce Order Analysis View Dashboard

Project Description

This project analyzes E-commerce sales performance using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard was built to idenitfy sales patterns, customer behavior, and top performing product. The dashboard inculde the following feactures:

  • Total quantity by product: A breakdown of how many quantities sold by each product.
  • Payment method: This compares the different payment method customers used in making payment.
  • Total revenue by product: Highlights the total revenue generated by each product.
  • Referral sources: Analyze the different customer acquistion channels.
  • Order statues by quantity and product: This explains how order statue affect different product and their quantity.

Tools Used

  • Microsoft Excel
  • Pivot Tables
  • Pivot Charts
  • Slicers
  • Timeline

Key Findings

  • Best Selling Products: Printers,tables and chair recorded the highest order quanutites and generated storng revenue compared to other products.
  • Payment Preference: Online payments were the most commonly used payment method, indicating a growing prefenence for conveinent digital transactions among customers.
  • Order Status Insight: A noticalbe number of cancelled and returned orders were ideentified, highlighting areas where delivery efficiency and customer statisfaction could be improved.
  • Customer Acquisition Channels: Social media and referral platforms contributed significantly to customer traffic, showing the importance of digital marketing channels in driving sales.
  • Product Demand Patterns: Certain products consistently performed better across different order statuses, helping identify high demand items and supporting smarter inventory planning.

This dashboard provide valuable business insight that can support decision making in sales monitoring, customer bevavior analysis, marketing strategy and operation improvemennt.
