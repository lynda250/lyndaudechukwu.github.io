📊 Excel

Medical Insurance Cost

Project Description

An exploratory analysis of a 7,500 policy holder medical insurance data, built in Microsoft Excel. The goal is to uncover the key drivers behind annual medical expenditure and segment the population by risk profile to support data driven underwriting and pricing decisions.

Tools Used

Microsoft ExcelPivot Tables Pivot ChartsSlicersPower Query

Key Findings

  • Smoking status emerged as the single strongest predictor of high medical costs across all age groups and regions.
  • BMI above 30 was consistently associated with elevated insurance charges, reinforcing obesity as a major cost driver.
  • Older policyholders (50+) incurred significantly higher costs, highlighting the need for age-tiered pricing strategies.
  • High-risk segments (smokers with high BMI) represented a small portion of the population but accounted for a disproportionate share of total expenditure.
  • Regional variation in costs was observed, with certain regions showing higher average charges regardless of individual risk factors.

📈 Power BI

Mental Health & Social Media Balance

Project Description

This Power BI project is a behavioral analytics report examining how 500 users across 6 social media platforms interact with digital technology and the downstream effects on lifestyle indicators like sleep, exercise, and screen time. The dashboard helps identify dependency patterns and potential mental health risk signals.

Tools Used

Power BIPower Query DAXKPI Tracking Data Visualization

Key Findings

  • 92% of users spend more than 4 hours daily on social media which shows a significant mental health risk indicator.
  • TikTok leads platform usage (95 users), edging out X/Twitter (88) and LinkedIn (87), with Instagram at the bottom (74).This narrow spread suggests users are distributed fairly evenly, though TIkTok and X/Twitter may warrant closer scrutiny given their algorithmically driven and high engagement design.
  • Exercise frequency peaks at 2–3 sessions per week, suggesting a sedentary lifestyle pattern when combined with high screen time.
  • Sleep quality peaks at around 6 hours for most users, very few user report 10 hours sleep suggesting most users cluster around moderate sleep duration potentially disrupted by late night screen use.
  • 57% of users went without social media for only 0–3 days, pointing to a high dependency pattern across the sample.
